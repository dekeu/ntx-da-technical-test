# Executive Summary — FY2026 Sales and Profitability

## Performance overview

FY2026 net revenue increased by **10.87%**, from **IDR 28.41 billion** to **IDR 31.50 billion**, while net quantity increased by **16.44%**. However, gross profit decreased by **10.62%**, from **IDR 4.14 billion** to **IDR 3.70 billion**, and gross margin fell from **14.57%** to **11.75%**. The business therefore generated more sales but earned less profit from them.

## Main findings

The decline was mainly associated with selling prices and discounting rather than a company-wide rise in unit costs. Average selling price per net unit fell by **4.78%**, compared with a **1.64%** reduction in average unit cost.

Laptop and Smartphone were the largest contributors to the profit decline, with gross profit falling by **IDR 362.1 million** and **IDR 212.4 million**, respectively. Laptop revenue increased by **IDR 1.54 billion**, but its gross margin fell from **7.50%** to **4.26%**. Because Laptop average unit cost also decreased, the available data does not support higher Laptop supplier costs as the main cause.

Online gross margin also dropped substantially, from **13.90%** to **5.75%**. In addition, FY2026 sales with discounts above 15% generated **IDR 3.59 billion** in revenue but produced a **gross loss of IDR 364.5 million**. These findings indicate that weaker pricing and high-discount sales were major areas of margin pressure.

## Three-month outlook

Estimated net revenue is **IDR 2.14 billion for October 2026**, **IDR 3.21 billion for November**, and **IDR 3.78 billion for December**. The forecast uses the same months in 2025 and applies the FY2026 annual revenue growth rate of 10.87%. The estimates assume that the same-month pattern from 2025 and growth rate continue; changes in demand, pricing, or promotions may produce different results.

## Recommended actions

1. **Test a 15% discount cap on Completed sales.** Estimated gross-profit improvement: **IDR 502.8 million**, assuming sales quantity does not decline. Returns and cancelled transactions remain unchanged.
2. **Promote Aksesoris and Peralatan Kantor.** Shifting 5% of revenue from Laptop and Smartphone toward these higher-margin categories (Aksesoris and Peralatan Kantor) has an estimated impact of **IDR 398.7 million**, assuming demand and category margins remain stable.
3. **Explore a 2% Laptop cost saving.** Estimated gross-profit improvement: **IDR 282.3 million**, assuming product quality and sales are unaffected.

These are independent what-if scenarios and should not be added together or treated as guaranteed results.

## Limitation

The analysis includes imputed costs from the cleaned dataset. The overall revenue-up, profit-down conclusion remains after excluding imputed-cost rows, but Networking requires caution because **60.55% of its FY2026 rows** contain imputed costs. The analysis identifies relationships in the data but does not prove that discounts caused the profit decline.
